ExploreJar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version. ExploreJar keeps the places you save so you can find them again. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Your saved places live on your phone, and, if you have an account, in a private online copy that only you can read.

This policy explains what ExploreJar (“the app”, “we”, “us”) does with your information. The app is made by Wanderfly Creative LTD.

What we collect, and why

  • Account details. If you create an account, we receive your sign-in details from Sign in with Apple (your name and email, or Apple's private relay email if you choose to hide it) or the email address you sign up with. We use this to identify your account and let you sign in on a new phone.
  • Your saved places. The places, collections, notes, tags and links you save. They are stored on your phone. With an account, a private copy is also stored online (Google Firebase) so you can restore it on a new phone. Only your account can read it.
  • Shared jars. If you share a collection with a friend, the collection's name, its places and the display names of its members are stored online so everyone in it can see and edit it. Anyone with the invite code can join, so only share the code with people you trust.
  • Links you paste or share. When you paste a link (for example a TikTok or YouTube video), the app reads the public page to find the places mentioned. Reading a TikTok link happens on your phone. We keep the link, the video's title and the places found so you can open the original later.
  • Screenshots you choose. If you pick a screenshot, the text in it is read on your phone and nothing is uploaded. Only the places you then save are kept.
  • Your location (optional). If you allow it, the app uses your location while you use it, to show what's nearby and sort places by distance. We don't store your location history.
  • Notifications (optional). If you turn them on, reminders are created on your phone.

Services we use

  • Apple Maps finds and describes places. When you search or open Discover, the search words and the area are sent to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) handles accounts and the online copy of your data, under Google's Firebase privacy terms.
  • Google Places may be used to show photos of places. This sends the place's name and position to Google.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and, if you subscribe, payments and subscription management. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or analytics that follow you across other apps and sites.
  • We don't read your contacts or your photo library. The app only sees a screenshot you pick yourself.

How long we keep it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or the app. Your online copy and any jars you own are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data. In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your online backup, the jars you own, your sign-in link with Apple and your account.
  • Turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Ask us. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by writing to us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, for example under the GDPR or the CCPA, and we will honor them.

Children

ExploreJar is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted, and each person's online copy is protected by access rules so that only their account can read it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
